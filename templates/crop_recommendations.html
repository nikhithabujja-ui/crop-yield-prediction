Crop Recommendations

Explore suitable environmental conditions for different crops.

Rice

Suitable for warm and humid conditions with good rainfall.

🌧️ Rainfall 1100–1300 mm
🌡️ Temperature 27–29 °C
💧 Humidity 78–82 %
🧪 Soil pH 6.2–6.7
🌾

Wheat

Performs well in moderate temperatures and rainfall.

🌧️ Rainfall 550–650 mm
🌡️ Temperature 21–23 °C
💧 Humidity 58–62 %
🧪 Soil pH 6.5–7.0
🌽

Maize

Suitable for warm temperatures and moderate rainfall.

🌧️ Rainfall 650–750 mm
🌡️ Temperature 24–26 °C
💧 Humidity 63–68 %
🧪 Soil pH 6.1–6.5
🌿

Cotton

Prefers warm conditions with moderate humidity.

🌧️ Rainfall 750–850 mm
🌡️ Temperature 29–31 °C
💧 Humidity 53–57 %
🧪 Soil pH 7.0–7.3
🎋

Sugarcane

Requires warm conditions, high rainfall and humidity.

🌧️ Rainfall 1300–1500 mm
🌡️ Temperature 26–28 °C
💧 Humidity 73–78 %
🧪 Soil pH 6.5–7.0
🥜

Groundnut

Suitable for warm temperatures and moderate rainfall.

🌧️ Rainfall 450–550 mm
🌡️ Temperature 27–29 °C
💧 Humidity 52–58 %
🧪 Soil pH 6.0–6.5
